# Tire & Wheel Product Improvements

## Overview
We've significantly enhanced the product cards and product pages to provide tire buyers (both retailers and end users) with comprehensive information they need to make informed purchasing decisions.

## Key Improvements

### 1. Enhanced Product Specifications
Added detailed technical specifications that tire buyers look for:

#### For Tires:
- **Tire Size**: Complete size specifications (e.g., 225/45R17)
- **Load Index & Speed Rating**: Load capacity and maximum speed capabilities
- **UTQG Ratings**: Treadwear, Traction, and Temperature grades
- **Season Type**: All-Season, Summer, Winter, Performance
- **Tire Type**: Grand Touring, Max Performance, Studless Ice & Snow, etc.
- **Warranty Information**: Coverage details and duration
- **Construction Type**: Radial construction details

#### For Wheels:
- **Rim Size**: Diameter and width (e.g., 17x9)
- **Bolt Pattern**: Lug pattern specifications (e.g., 5x114.3)
- **Offset**: Wheel offset measurements
- **Finish**: Color and surface treatment details
- **Wheel Type**: Alloy, Steel, Cast, Forged

#### For Accessories:
- **Thread Size**: Compatibility specifications
- **Sensor Type**: TPMS details
- **Material Specifications**: Construction materials
